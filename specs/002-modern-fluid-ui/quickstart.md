# Quickstart: Verify Modern Fluid UI

1. Open the app on a smartphone (mobile viewport).
2. Toggle theme to Dark; confirm immediate update and persistence across reload.
3. Change language to Greek; confirm all visible text and dates update without reload.
4. Scroll through pages with lists; observe smooth in-view animations without blocking input.
5. Enable reduced motion at OS level; reload and confirm animations are minimized/disabled. Then, open the Settings panel and toggle "Reduced motion" to verify user override works regardless of system setting.
6. Confirm color contrast and focus indicators meet WCAG AA in both themes.
